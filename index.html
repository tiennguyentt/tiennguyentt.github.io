02 / AI-Native

I engineered the workflow, not just adopted the tools.

Most PMs prompt a chatbot and paste the output. I built a system: agents draft the specs, a second model independently verifies them, and every requirement is traceable to its source before it ships. The agents do the drafting; I read the diff and own the call.

  • Two-model peer pipeline. One model drafts the spec, a second model independently reviews and verifies, and they converge by evidence rank before anything ships. This site was designed by that same Claude + Codex loop.
  • Transcript-to-wiki knowledge base. The raw meeting transcript stays the immutable source of truth, an LLM maintains a living wiki on top of it, and a schema flags contradictions against the transcript before anything reaches review.
  • Custom PM agents and skills. Purpose-built agents for spec grading, requirement drafting (BRD / FRD / SRS), and ticket generation, working against live Jira and Drive state, never a stale snapshot.
  • Provenance tags and quality gates. Every requirement carries a provenance tag back to its source, and automated gates block anything that doesn't pass. Specs arrive with source links, acceptance criteria, and review notes, so engineering has fewer questions to ask.
